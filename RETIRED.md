# Retired 2026-08-23

This was a fork of [mike32snake/premier-law-site](https://github.com/mike32snake/premier-law-site),
made 2026-02-25. The `premier.law` domain was pointed at the fork rather than at
the original, so for five months the public site was served from here while
other work continued upstream. The two drifted apart, and edits that only
existed here (the FormSubmit contact form, privacy policy, favicons, the full
legal disclaimer, Derek's education credentials) were nearly lost when the
upstream copy was promoted.

That is fixed. Everything from this fork was merged upstream, and `premier.law`
now points at the original repo.

**The live site is https://premier.law, served from
[mike32snake/premier-law-site](https://github.com/mike32snake/premier-law-site).**

Nothing here is current. Do not edit or deploy this repo.
